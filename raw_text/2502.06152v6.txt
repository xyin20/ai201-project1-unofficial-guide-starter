1 INTRODUCTION

workflows in which human experts and AI models are paired for decision-making are sought in
medicine, finance, and law, among others. Statistical models can often exceed the accuracy of
human experts on average (Ægisdóttir et al., 2006; Grove et al., 2000; Meehl, 1954). However,
whenever humans have access to additional information over an AI model, there is potential to
achieve complementary performance by pairing the two, i.e., better performance than either the
humanorAIalone. Forexample,aphysicianmayhaveaccesstoinformationthatisnotcapturedin
structuredhealthrecords(Aluretal.,2024).
